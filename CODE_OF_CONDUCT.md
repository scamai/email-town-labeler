# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any other channel where people represent the project.

## Summary

- **Be respectful.** Welcome people of every background and experience level. Disagree about
  ideas, not people.
- **Be considerate.** Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; take responsibility
  for mistakes and learn from them.
- **Not acceptable:** harassment, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
  derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private
  information (including their email) without explicit permission.
- **Enforcement.** Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily
  or permanently ban contributors who violate this code, following the Covenant's enforcement
  guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers at **security@scam.ai** (subject line "Code of Conduct"). All reports are reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
